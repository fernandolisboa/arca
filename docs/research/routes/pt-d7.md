- **Findings:**
  - Confirmed, unchanged: the means scale of 100%, 50% and 30% of the minimum wage net of social security contributions (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 2.º, n.º 2; EUR 920, Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025; 11%, Código dos Regimes Contributivos, article 53.º); the spouse rule with both spouses 18 or over (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 99.º, n.º 6); the minor children and the dependent parents (article 99.º, n.º 1, b), c) and f); the exclusion of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens (article 4.º); the leap-day convention. The `household-income` requirement now also cites Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 24.º-B, n.º 1, b), the provision that applies the scale to the family members who apply with the principal.
  - Missing, now an assessment `means-for-12-months`: the means must be assured for at least 12 months, a retiree proving the pension and that its payment is guaranteed, others the income and its availability in Portugal (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 5.º, n.º 6).
  - Missing, now the assessments `no-entry-ban` and `criminal-record`: no visa during an entry ban or under a return or refusal alert (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 52.º, n.º 1, a) to c)), nor after a conviction for a crime Portugal punishes with more than one year of prison (n.º 3). No case fact records any of it, and an assessment must name a fact as evidence, so both show the principal's visa refusals, the nearest fact there is.
  - Missing, now possible dependents: the partner in a de facto union and the partner's unmarried minor or incapable children entrusted to the partner (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 24.º-B, n.º 2, which adds article 100.º, n.º 1, of the law to the family of article 58.º, n.º 5). A de facto union is one of more than two years (Lei n.º 7/2001, article 1.º, n.º 2), so the rule asks for two years and a day from the start the case records, and both partners must be 18 or over (article 99.º, n.º 6, which names the spouse "ou equiparado" and the de facto union). Whether the union is duly proven and recognized, and whether a child is entrusted to the partner, the case does not record: such people show as possible.
  - Missing, now possible dependents: adult children who depend on the couple, when incapable (article 99.º, n.º 1, b) or single and studying at a school in Portugal (n.º 1, d). Neither fact is recorded.
  - The children rules' titles now say what is not checked: an adoption must be recognized by Portugal (article 99.º, n.º 1, c), and a child of only one spouse needs the other parent's consent or a custody decision (n.º 4).
  - Missing, now costs: EUR 110 for processing a national visa application (table of consular fees, article 15.º, n.º 4, as set by Portaria n.º 91/2025/1, in force 2025-03-11; Lei n.º 23/2007, article 209.º, n.º 1), for a principal and each family member aged 18 or over; a possible cost for anyone aged 6 to 17, whom the consulate may exempt, and none for a child under 6 (article 22.º, n.º 1). The section of the table that holds visas is not among those updated each March. A consulate charges in euros or local currency at the Banco de Portugal's rate (article 28.º). Not applied: the exemption for descendants of residence permit holders under family reunification (article 22.º, n.º 1.1, i), since this route's principal holds no permit yet, and the one for family members of EU or Portuguese citizens (n.º 1.1, f), which goes with the open question on such principals.
  - Missing, now durations: the visa decision within 60 days and the visa's four months and two entries (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 58.º, n.os 4 and 2); the first residence permit's two years (article 75.º, n.º 1). The last two are how long a document is valid, not a wait.
  - Sources: split by provision, so Lei n.º 23/2007 and the portaria appear as several sources with the same address. `lei-23-2007` keeps the family of article 58.º, n.º 5, with article 99.º, n.os 1 and 4, which the family rules read together.
  - Gaps kept, each in [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md):
    - The residence permit's own fees. Portaria n.º 307/2023 sets them, updates them each 1 March by the consumer price index and cuts them 25% for applications through the digital channel; the updated amounts are not in the gazette, and aima.gov.pt is not readable, so no current amount has an official source.
    - The residence permit's own conditions (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 77.º, n.º 1), accommodation and registration with social security among them: they belong to the stage after arrival, which no route models yet.
    - Minor siblings under the principal's guardianship (article 99.º, n.º 1, g): the case has no path to a sibling.
    - A minor child counts without a test of dependence: article 99.º, n.º 1, b), speaks of minor children "a cargo" of the couple or of one spouse, and the rules, as in version 3, take every child under 18 to be in their parents' charge.
    - An adult child of the principal alone who depends only on the principal's spouse is left out: the child rule reads dependence on the principal, and only a child of the spouse is asked about dependence on the spouse.
    - The partner's children show as possible even when the partner is under 18: a two-step path cannot read the partner's age.
    - A dependent adult child counts 30% in the means scale (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 2.º, n.º 2, c), and the requirement counts every adult at 50%. Such a child is only ever possible, so this widens the upper bound of the amount and never turns a result into "eligible" or "not eligible" wrongly.
    - A possible dependent makes the amount an interval: an income between its bounds reads "Faltam informações" with no fact to ask for.
    - The family's means are asked for the stay applied for or 12 months, whichever is less (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 24.º-B, n.º 1, b); the 12 months assessment speaks only of the principal.
    - The statement of responsibility that replaces proof of means for nationals of states under the CPLP agreement (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 12.º-A), and principals who are family members of EU citizens, both already open.
    - The other refusal grounds of article 52.º, a danger to public order, security or health (n.º 4) and an earlier illegal entry or stay (n.os 10 and 11), are not modeled: no fact records them. Its documents (a valid travel document, travel insurance) and the criminal record certificate asked from age 16 (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 12.º, n.os 1, c), and 7) belong to the document checklist, not to this route's rules.
  - The cases file starts from version 3's cases. The extended family now has possible members, so its cases changed: `extended-family-income-at-the-net-threshold` became `extended-family-income-at-the-lower-bound` and reads "missing information", and two cases pin the upper bound. Every case that meets the requirements now reads "needs assessment". New cases cover a couple with a child at the net threshold, the partner (three years, one year, exactly two years, two years and a day, a partner under 18, a principal under 18, a married child of the partner) and the adult children (the 18th birthday and the day before, no dependence, dependence on the spouse). R8 has no cost expectations, so the fee is pinned only by the hashes and this record.
  - The independent check cannot read the consolidated texts, which are script-drawn pages, and reads a PDF only after [#363](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/363), so its report on version 4 will be incomplete until then.
