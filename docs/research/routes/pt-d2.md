# pt-d2

Research notes for Portugal's D2 residence visa for independent professional activity or for immigrant entrepreneurs, route `pt-d2`. It has no file in `packages/knowledge/data`: it entered the catalog through a session review ([ADR-0032, amendment 2026-11-25](../../adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md#amendment-2026-11-25-new-routes)). The seed research is in [portugal.md](../portugal.md#residence-visas); the other Portuguese routes are [`pt-d7`](pt-d7.md) and [`pt-d8`](pt-d8.md), whose family rules, fees, durations and general documents this route shares.
