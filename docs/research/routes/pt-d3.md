# pt-d3

Research notes for Portugal's D3 residence visa for teaching, highly qualified or cultural activity, route `pt-d3`. It has no file in `packages/knowledge/data`: it entered the catalog through a session review ([ADR-0032, amendment 2026-11-25](../../adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md#amendment-2026-11-25-new-routes)). The seed research is in [portugal.md](../portugal.md#residence-visas); the other Portuguese routes are [`pt-d2`](pt-d2.md), [`pt-d7`](pt-d7.md) and [`pt-d8`](pt-d8.md), whose family rules, fees, durations and general documents this route shares.

## Audits

### 2026-11-25, session review (new route)

The [session rule review](../../runbooks/session-rule-review.md) that wrote the route.

- **Version written:** 1, from 2026-01-01, stored active by the review's pull request. What it holds, the rounds and the leftover list are in [the review's record](../../../packages/knowledge/reviews/2026-11-pt-d3/README.md).
- **Watch next:** the 2027 minimum wage ([#220](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/220)) changes the means assessment's amounts, so when it comes, version 2 from 2027-01-01 must follow `pt-d7` and `pt-d8`, keeping version 1's documents; a Council of Ministers resolution listing the shortage professions of article 61.º-A, n.º 2; any change to the Tech Visa rules (Portaria n.º 328/2018); the EU Blue Card, a residence permit of its own (articles 121.º-A ff.) that a future route may model; an official page with the consulates' own D3 list (`vistos.mne.gov.pt`), once a host for it is on the official-domain list and readable.
