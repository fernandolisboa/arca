# Brazilian side: selling assets, moving money, leaving

Checked 2026-10-31.

## Capital gains on real estate (individuals)

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Progressive rates on the gain: 15% up to BRL 5 million, 17.5% up to BRL 10 million, 20% up to BRL 30 million, 22.5% above | [Adriano Freire](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/ganho-capital-venda-imovel-aliquotas-isencoes-2026) |
| Older properties have reduction factors | [Adriano Freire](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/ganho-capital-venda-imovel-aliquotas-isencoes-2026) |
| Tax due by the last business day of the month after the sale | [CNB/SP](https://cnbsp.org.br/2026/03/12/e-investidor-venda-de-imovel-no-imposto-de-renda-2026-quem-precisa-pagar-e-quando-ha-isencao/) |

## FX tax (IOF) on outbound transfers

| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| 1.1% when the transfer's nature is investment abroad; 3.5% for transfers to the sender's own account for availability | [Safra](https://oespecialista.safra.com.br/iof-no-cambio-remessas-exterior-2026/), [Cescon Barrieu](https://cesconbarrieu.com.br/alteracoes-no-iof-para-cambio-entenda-as-novas-regras/) |

The difference matters at scale: on USD 3 million, classifying the transfer wrongly costs about USD 72,000.

## Source-of-funds trail (per asset)

Documents an investment route will ask for, in order: title registration and purchase deed; income tax returns showing ownership and cost; sale contract and proof of receipt; capital-gains tax payment; gift deed and state gift tax (ITCMD) when money passes to another person; FX contract; transfer to an account in the investor's name; the investment itself.
