## Residence visas

D7 and D8 are now route versions `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` in `packages/knowledge/data/routes`, verified against the Diário da República on 2026-11-05. Version 2 of each, added on 2026-11-07, corrects version 1 from its first day: Lei n.º 23/2007 does not apply to nationals of EU or EEA states or of Switzerland (article 4.º, n.º 2, a), Portugal included, so a principal who holds any of those citizenships is not eligible. Version 3 of each, added on 2026-11-07 after the country guide's reading of the same texts, corrects version 2 from its first day: the portaria measures the means test against the minimum wage net of social security contributions (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 2.º, n.º 2), so it uses EUR 818.80, 89% of EUR 920 after the worker's 11% (Código dos Regimes Contributivos, article 53.º), and a spouse counts only when both spouses are 18 or over (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 99.º, n.º 6). D8's own income floor of four minimum wages (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 31.º-A) stays gross. Version 4 of each, added on 2026-11-11 by the first full audit ([pt-d7](routes/pt-d7.md), [pt-d8](routes/pt-d8.md)), corrects version 3 from its first day: it adds the EUR 110 consular fee for a national visa, the visa's and the first permit's durations, the assessments the law leaves to the consulate (12 months of assured means, the remote work link for D8, entry bans and convictions), and the partner in a de facto union of more than two years, the partner's children and dependent adult children as possible family members. Where they differ from these notes, the route versions win: the means test counts 50% of the minimum wage for every adult after the principal (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 2.º, which gives a dependent adult child 30%; the [open question](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) on the Portuguese routes has the difference), and D8 also needs the household means test. Version 7 of each, stored on 2026-11-20 by a session review ([its record](../../packages/knowledge/reviews/2026-11-pt/README.md)), corrects version 6 from its first day: a spouse shows as possible when either spouse is under 18, a D7 family that reaches the means only with income from work reads as possible, and the entry ban is quoted in the wording in force.

D2 is route `pt-d2` since a session review of 2026-11-25 ([its record](../../packages/knowledge/reviews/2026-11-pt-d2/README.md), [route notes](routes/pt-d2.md)): version 1, from 2026-01-01, decides only the EU, EEA and Swiss exclusion and leaves the paths (a services contract in a liberal profession, an investment made, means in Portugal with an intent to invest, or a certified incubator under the Startup Visa rules) and the means to assessments, and shares `pt-d8` version 8's family rules, fees, durations and general documents.

D3 is route `pt-d3` since a session review of 2026-11-25 ([its record](../../packages/knowledge/reviews/2026-11-pt-d3/README.md), [route notes](routes/pt-d3.md)): version 1, from 2026-01-01, decides only the EU, EEA and Swiss exclusion and leaves the paths (article 61.º: a contract, an invitation letter or a Tech Visa statement of responsibility; article 61.º-A: a highly qualified employee's contract with a pay floor) and the means to assessments, and shares `pt-d2` version 1's family rules, fees, durations and general documents, with article 61.º's 30-day decision.

Every version uses the 2026 guaranteed minimum monthly wage (RMMG), EUR 920 (Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025), net of social security from version 3. On 2026-11-06 the Diário da República had no decree setting the RMMG for 2027, so the next versions of `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` (v9, after the v2 to v7 corrections and v8's document list) wait for it ([#220](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/220)); on 2026-11-11 and on 2026-11-20 there was still none. The last decrees came out on 2023-11-17 ([DL 107/2023](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/107-2023-224427489)), 2024-12-19 ([DL 112/2024](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/112-2024-900706889)) and 2025-12-29 ([DL 139/2025](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/139-2025-992879809)), checked the same day.

## Family reunification

Checked on 2026-11-05 against the consolidated Lei n.º 23/2007: the two-year wait is article 98.º, n.º 1, as amended by Lei n.º 61/2025, with the exceptions of n.º 3. Adult children qualify only when they are incapable (article 99.º, n.º 1, b) or study in Portugal (n.º 1, d), except for holders under article 90.º-A. Not yet a route version. Since #513 the wait can be expressed as `covers` over `principal.residencePeriods` where `residencePeriod.country` is `PT` and `residencePeriod.basis` is `permanent_residence` or `temporary_residence`, with `atLeast` `P2Y`; whether the stretch must be continuous, and how the n.º 3 exceptions apply, is still to be read from article 98.º when the route version is written.
