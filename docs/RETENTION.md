# Retention schedule

As of 2026-11-06. Decided in [ADR-0017](adr/0017-data-lifecycle-and-backups.md). Every row names how the period is enforced. A new kind of data, or a new processor, adds a row here in the same change, together with its entry in the personal-data registry and the data-flow table in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#data-flows).

## In our systems

| Data | Kept for | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Case data and account data | While the account exists; after 3 years without a sign-in, an email notice, then deletion 60 days later | Inactivity job (daily `enforceRetention` workflow), which records `deletion requested` (`inactivity`) with the notice; a sign-in before the deletion cancels it (`deletion cancelled`, `sign_in`); `eraseAccount` erases it when due |
| Account after a deletion request | 7 days locked, then erased | `eraseAccount` workflow |
| Case rows (`cases.*`: people, profiles, finances, relationships, assets and shares) | As long as their case and their keeper's account; a person goes when their keeper leaves the case or is erased, unless handed over first; the case goes with its last member | Cascades from `cases.member` and `cases.person`; the asset and empty-case triggers; `eraseAccount` deletes the member row first |
| Sessions | 3 days idle, 30 days at most | Better Auth expiry (slid only while the session is under 30 days old, so it ends by day 33 at the latest) plus a daily cleanup job; never backed up |
| Pending document uploads (new documents and replacements never confirmed) | 24 hours, with their objects | Daily cleanup job |
| Deleted and replaced document ciphertext | Deleted at once; the object's pathname is kept, without the document, until every upload grant for it has ended (1 hour), and the object is deleted again then, in case a late upload recreated it | Daily cleanup job |
| Browser and system families seen per passkey (`auth.passkey_client`) | While the passkey exists | Deleted with the passkey (foreign key cascade); erased with the account; backed up |
| Unconfirmed invited accounts | Erased when the inviter declines them, or when `confirmed_at` is still empty 30 days after creation | The decline, or the daily cleanup job, ends the sessions and marks the account due with a `deletion requested` event (`invite_declined` or `invite_unconfirmed`); the decline starts `eraseAccount` at once, and the daily `eraseAccount` runs pick up the rest |
| Account links (inviter and invitee) | While both accounts exist | Registry |
| Step-up challenges (`auth.step_up_challenge`) | 2 minutes, single use | Expiry check; deleted when verified; expired ones are swept on every new challenge and by the daily cleanup job; erased with the session or account; never backed up |
| Stored step-up assertions (`auth.step_up`) | As long as the record they prove (for example, an account deletion request), and erased with the account; one never spent is deleted after 15 minutes, when no action can spend it; a spent one stays with the account until the first consumer that keeps its own copy (such as the deletion request, spec 0004) lets spent ones be swept | Swept on every new challenge; cascade on account erasure; registry; backed up |
| Waits of added and recovery passkeys (`auth.passkey_activation`, a credential ID, a time and whether a recovery made it) | 24 hours, then active, or cancelled | Deleted at the account's first signed-in request after the wait, or with the passkey (foreign key cascade); erased with the account; backed up, so a restore keeps the wait |
| Add-passkey challenges (`auth.passkey_challenge`) | 2 minutes, single use | Expiry check; deleted by the attempt that spends it; expired ones are swept on every new challenge; erased with the session or account; never backed up |
| Recovery links (`auth.recovery_link`: a token hash, an account ID, times and a 2-minute challenge) | 30 minutes, single use | Deleted by the passkey it registers, by a lockout, by the account's next link; expired ones are swept on every new link and by the daily cleanup job; erased with the account; never backed up |
| Recovery lockouts (`auth.recovery_lock`: an account ID, a count of wrong codes and a lock end) | While the account exists; the right code resets the count, and a lock lasts 24 hours | The daily cleanup job deletes ended locks, whose count is already reset; cascade on account erasure; backed up |
| Owner-enrollment registration contexts (`auth.registration_context`, with the address the link went to) | 30 minutes, single use | Expiry check; deleted when a registration spends it; a new owner link deletes earlier ones and expired ones; the daily cleanup job; never backed up |
| Invites (`auth.invite`, with the invitee's address and the token's hash) | 48 hours unaccepted; once accepted, until the inviter confirms or declines the account, or 30 days, then with the accounts | Expiry check; an expired invite never accepted is deleted, with an `invite expired` event, whenever any account sends an invite, and by the daily cleanup job; a new invite to the same address replaces the open one; erased with the inviter's or the invitee's account; backed up, since an accepted invite is the lasting link between the accounts. |
| Passkey ceremony state (`auth.verification`: a challenge with a registration context's ID and address, or a signed-in account's ID) | 5 minutes, single use | Expiry check; deleted by the ceremony that spends it; expired ones are swept whenever a new ceremony starts and by account erasure; never backed up |
| Rule summaries (`knowledge.rule_summary`: the digest of what the model read, a prompt version, a model and the model's plain-text summary; no personal data) | Forever; a row stops being read once the prompt, the model or what the model reads (the version or its rule cases) changes, and the catalog bounds the rows to a few per version | Never erased; on the registry's unowned list, with no personal data ([ADR-0025](adr/0025-rule-summary-for-the-approver.md)) |
| Spent enrollment codes (`auth.setup`, a hash, no personal data) | Forever, so an emptied user table never revives a code | Never erased; on the registry's unowned list, with no personal data; backed up |
| Account recovery code (`auth.recovery_code`, a salted hash) | While the account exists; replaced by each new kit, and one a recovery issued lapses with its link unless the passkey registers; cancelling a recovery deletes it | Replaced in place; cascade on account erasure; backed up |
| What a passkey's authenticator reported at registration (`auth.passkey_capability`) | While the passkey exists | Deleted with the passkey (foreign key cascade); erased with the account; backed up |
| Notification channels (push subscriptions) | Until revoked, reported gone by the push service, or the account is deleted | Delivery step and registry |
| Notification messages and their deliveries | 90 days | Daily cleanup job |
| Rule reports (`cases.rule_report`: a route version, a requirement, a reason and an optional note) | 365 days, or until the reporter leaves the case | Daily cleanup job; cascade when the reporter's membership ends; erased with the account |
| Comparison preferences (`cases.comparison_preferences`: one member's weights, star ratings, names of their own criteria and hidden countries) | While the member is in the case | Replaced in place by each write; cascade when the membership ends; erased with the account; in the data export; backed up |
| Vault envelopes, recipients, sharing state and document rows | While the account exists; a rotated identity's envelopes until every sharing owner re-encrypts, or 90 days | Registry; daily cleanup job for rotated envelopes |
| Rate-limit counters (`auth.rate_limit`, HMAC keys only) | Their window (up to an hour; a day for invites, rule reports, free-text intake and rule summaries) | A hit after the window starts a new one; the daily `/internal/cron/rate-limit-sweep` deletes ended windows in production; previews run no cron, so their ended windows stay until the next hit on the same key, bounded by the routes and addresses that reached them; never backed up |
| Non-production email outbox (`auth.outbox`) | 7 days | Each outbox write deletes expired rows, and the preview page shows only unexpired ones; the daily cleanup job deletes the rest |
| Audit log | 5 years; records hold identifiers and event codes only | Purge job, specified before the first record turns 5 (2031); it keeps each account's latest `recovery_kit_regenerated`, `recovery_completed`, `recovery_cancelled`, `vault_created`, `vault_recovery_resealed`, `vault_identity_rotated` and active `passkey_revoked`, which decide whether the vault needs a new recovery secret or a rotation (spec 0005, R2 and R4) |
| Knowledge decisions (`knowledge.decision`: an admin's account ID, the route or comparison version decided, its proof, and for decisions made before [ADR-0027](adr/0027-admins-and-the-checked-batch-activation.md) the step-up assertion with its passkey's public key) | Forever, as the record of which rule and comparison versions are in use | Insert-only; never erased, so an admin's erasure keeps them; on the registry as retained; backed up |
| Admin grants (insert-only rows: grantee and granting account IDs, time, given or taken away, and the step-up a grant spent) | Forever, as decisions that rely on them are | Insert-only; never erased, so an account's erasure keeps its account IDs here as it does on decisions, which need the grant history to count; on the registry as retained; backed up ([ADR-0027](adr/0027-admins-and-the-checked-batch-activation.md)) |
| Independent check starts, progress and reports (`knowledge.check_start`: route, version and time; `knowledge.check_progress`: per start, the stages its run recorded, with counts, cited official URLs, item keys with their verified status, model token counts and fixed codes; `knowledge.independent_check`: route, version, hashes, prompt, model, items with quotes from official pages and the fetch log; no personal data) | Forever; a report stops being read once the version, guide, prompt or model changes, or after 30 days; automatic starts add at most 15 rows a day and an admin's own starts one row per tap, and progress is read for the newest start of the last 24 hours and, for its cost, for the version's newest start with a report | Never erased; on the registry's unowned list, with no personal data ([ADR-0027](adr/0027-admins-and-the-checked-batch-activation.md)) |
| Data exports | Not stored; built per request | — |

## At processors

| Processor | Data | Kept for | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neon | Deleted rows in history | 6 hours (Free plan history window) | Neon project setting |
| Neon | Incident evidence branches (`incident-YYYYMMDD`) and the backup branch a point-in-time restore leaves: full copies of the database | Until the investigation and any ANPD process close | Deleted by the owner as the [incident runbook](runbooks/incident-response.md#take-an-evidence-branch) says; their app roles are set to `NOLOGIN` when taken |
| Neon | A manual snapshot the owner took | Until the owner deletes it | Neon console |
| Vercel Blob (São Paulo) | Document ciphertext | While the document exists; a replaced or deleted blob may be served from the CDN for up to 60 seconds | Deleted with the document ([ADR-0021](adr/0021-stay-on-vercel-and-neon.md)) |
| Vercel | Runtime logs (allow-listed fields only) | 1 day | Vercel Pro policy |
| Vercel Workflows | Run payloads (identifiers only) | Unreadable at run end, deleted within 36 days | `experimental_retention: 0` ([ADR-0009](adr/0009-background-jobs-guarantees.md)) |
| Vercel Workflows | Run metadata (no personal data) | Up to 1 year | Vercel policy |
| Sentry (United States) | Scrubbed errors and traces | 30 days | Developer plan policy |
| Resend | Recipient address, message content and metadata (United States) | 30 days on the Free, Pro and Scale plans ([Resend pricing](https://resend.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-04) | Vendor policy |
| Proton Mail (the owner's mailbox) | Members' replies to Arca's email (Switzerland) | Until the owner deletes them | Owner |
| Langfuse (EU) | Model-call metadata: no prompt or completion text, no account, case or document IDs, so nothing to erase per person | Set when Langfuse is adopted ([ADR-0016](adr/0016-observability-and-scrubbing.md)) | — |
