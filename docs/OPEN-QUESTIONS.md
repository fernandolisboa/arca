- [ ] One active passkey controls the account (ADR-0015 residual): decide before the real-data gate whether revoking passkeys, regenerating the kit and cancelling recovery need the designated adult or a 24-hour cancellable delay.
- [ ] Renamed states in citizenship and ancestry facts: ISO 3166-3 also retires codes of states that still exist under a new name (`BUMM` to `MM`, `DYBJ` to `BJ`, `ZRCD` to `CD`). Decide, before the first descent route, whether a rule naming the current code also matches the retired one, as a reviewed mapping in the knowledge base ([spec 0002](specs/0002-rules-engine-and-knowledge-base.md#r7-countries)).
- [ ] Former states in an ancestor's residence: only citizenship facts admit ISO 3166-3 codes today, so a rule cannot match an ancestor's last residence in a former state. Decide with the first descent route whether `lineage.ancestor.residenceCountry` admits them.
- [ ] Unconfirmed citizenship by descent in the snapshot: a parent who is Polish or Italian by descent but never confirmed is usually recorded with only the citizenship they use, and the citizenship facts read a recorded list as complete. `pl-descent` v1 leaves such a link open below the ancestor; `it-descent` v1 still reads it as no citizenship. Decide whether the snapshot should mark a citizenship as claimed but unconfirmed, so every descent route reads it the same way.
- [ ] How long a citizenship has been held: the Mercosur Residence Agreements count a naturalized national only after five years (art. 2 of the agreements approved by Uruguay's Ley 17.927), and no fact reads a citizenship's age, so `uy-mercosur-permanent` v1 reads every listed citizenship as enough. Argentina's migration directorate also asks five years of a naturalized national, so `ar-mercosur-temporary` v1 shows a non-native citizenship of a listed state as possible. `py-mercosur-temporary` and `cl-mercosur-temporary` v1 do the same, reading art. 2 of Paraguay's Ley 3578, which Chile's Decreto 177/2022, art. 46, applies through the agreement's own terms. Decide whether to add a fact such as the years a citizenship has been held, read from its `acquired` date and `basis`.
- [ ] Who counts as a family member under the Mercosur Residence Agreements (art. 9.2, Uruguay's Ley 17.927): `uy-mercosur-permanent` v1 includes a dependent unmarried adult child and stepchild, while Ley 18.250 art. 10 lists only minor children and adults with a disability. Confirm with the migration directorate's practice, and record a Uruguayan convention for counting ages (leap-day birthdays) so a later version can test for minors. Paraguay's migration law (Ley 6984, art. 4.10) lists parents, spouses, unmarried minor children and adult children with a disability, and no partner, so `py-mercosur-temporary` v1 has no partner rule; decide whether a Paraguayan stable union (Ley 1/1992) should count, and whether art. 9.2 binds Paraguay for principals from Peru, Colombia and Ecuador, which acceded later (the agreement's art. 2 defines "Estados Partes" as "Estados miembros y Países asociados del MERCOSUR", which argues for including them).
- [ ] Facts no route can read yet for family members: an adult child's disability (`es-non-lucrative`, `uy-mercosur-permanent`, `ar-mercosur-temporary`, `py-mercosur-temporary` and `cl-mercosur-temporary` v1 show adult children as possible), whether a child under 24 studies at an institution Chile recognizes (`cl-mercosur-temporary` v1), when a couple began living together and whether their union is registered (`ar-mercosur-temporary` v1 counts a partner's two years from the start of the union the case records, `uy-mercosur-permanent` v1 its five), and whether a person under 18 was ever married (Argentina's Civil and Commercial Code, art. 27, emancipates by marriage for good). Decide whether the snapshot should record them.
- [ ] A principal's means for dependents with no amount in law: Chile's migration law (Ley 21.325, art. 74) requires a temporary resident to prove stable income enough to support their dependents, measured against the Social Development Ministry's indicators (Decreto 177/2022, art. 74). `cl-mercosur-temporary` v1 counts the spouse, the partner and minor children as dependents without that proof, because a dependent rule that can only answer "not known" fails the R8 coverage check ([spec 0002](specs/0002-rules-engine-and-knowledge-base.md#r8-table-driven-cases-fixtures-and-families)). Decide between reading the indicator as a sourced amount that changes over time, exempting such a rule from R8's "includes someone" check (as [#219](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/219) proposes for requirements), or a principal-level `unmodeled` condition that applies to every dependent.
- [ ] A version that must stop applying on a known date: `us-eb5` v2's capital amounts hold only for petitions filed before 2027-01-01, and the schema has no end date, so without a new version by then the route keeps showing USD 800,000. Decide before 2027-01-01 (if DHS has not published the amounts, or Portugal the 2027 minimum wage) between an end date in the schema and an `unmodeled` requirement exempted from the R8 "true at least once" coverage rule ([#219](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/219)). `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` v1 have the same gap: their income thresholds use the 2026 minimum wage, which a new decree has replaced every 1 January ([#220](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/220)). That decree has come out in November or December, so a new version can usually be ready before 1 January.
- [ ] Langfuse retention, set when it is adopted ([ADR-0016](adr/0016-observability-and-scrubbing.md)).
