# Session review 2026-11-lv-remote-work

A session review that adds a route ([ADR-0032, amendment 2026-11-25](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md#amendment-2026-11-25-new-routes), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)). Latvia held one route, `lv-capital-investment`. This review writes `lv-remote-work` version 1, from 2026-09-15 (the day the new Immigration Law took effect), on the `lv` official-domain list `lv-capital-investment` already pins, with the `eu-free-movement` country set at number 2. Every source was read on likumi.lv on 2026-11-25, São Paulo date: the Immigration Law (id 370622: arts. 1, 16 to 20, 22 and transitional provision 3), Cabinet Regulations No. 676 of 2011, the Visa Regulations (id 235770, kept in force by transitional provision 3, item 16, until new regulations and 1 December 2027 at the latest), No. 731 of 2024 on migration service fees, No. 225 of 2017 on financial means, No. 656 of 2015 on the minimum wage, No. 591 of 2008 on foreigners' health insurance and the Official Language Law. The acts issued under the new law were checked: the only one, Cabinet Regulations No. 669 of 10 November 2026 on entry restrictions, keeps long-stay visa applications open to citizens of Belarus (point 5.1) and stops Russian citizens' applications, whom the law already excludes.

## The route

A long-stay (type D) visa for someone who stays in Latvia while working remotely for an employer registered in another OECD member state, or as a self-employed person registered in an OECD member state (Immigration Law, art. 17 point 6). It lasts up to one year; one more may be requested, and after it ends no new long-stay visa on the same ground for six months (art. 18(2)).

- **Requirements the case decides.** Not a citizen of an EU or EEA state or of Switzerland (art. 1 point 29); not a citizen of Russia (art. 18(3)); and, from the work periods, an employee of the same employer seated outside Latvia for at least the last six months, still today (Regulations No. 676, point 19.10). A self-employed person, a contractor or a business owner reads as possible: the case does not record where a self-employed person is registered.
- **Assessments.** The employer's registration in another OECD member state, or the self-employed person's in an OECD member state; pay of at least 2.5 times Latvia's average monthly gross wage of the previous year (a figure the Central Statistical Bureau publishes, not on the `lv` list); that the work can be done remotely; means of subsistence (the minimum monthly wage, EUR 780 in 2026, for a stay over 30 days); health insurance of at least EUR 42,600 and what it covers; the extra check for citizens of the states Regulations No. 554 list (kept in force by transitional provision 3, item 8) and for people stateless or protected abroad (art. 9(1)); the refusal grounds of art. 22(1); and where and when to file.
- **Family.** None. The law and the Visa Regulations give a family member no visa on this ground, so the route has no dependent rules; each family member needs a ground of their own.
- **Costs.** The EUR 90 state fee for the long-stay visa request (Regulations No. 731, point 2.1); EUR 180 when filed in Latvia less than three working days before the lawful stay ends is in the title.
- **Durations.** The visa (up to one year), the second visa (up to one year) and the decision (15 days, up to 60 with more checks, in the title).
- **Documents.** Point 19 of the Visa Regulations: travel document, application form, photograph, health insurance policy copy, documents on the place of stay and on financial means (valid three months, Regulations No. 225, point 4), a ticket both ways or money for fuel (point 9), and the remote work documents of point 19.10, for an employee or for a self-employed person. Translations are `certified` on every document but the travel document, the form and the photograph (Official Language Law, art. 10(3)). New kinds: `employment_record`, `self_employment_registration` and `travel_ticket`. No document guide entry is added: neither is a Brazilian civil record the guide covers.

## Rounds

Three adversarial rounds. Round 1 had two reviewers, one reading every item against its provision, one looking on likumi.lv for anything that makes the route out of date or incomplete (amendments, acts issued under the new law, the law's redaction for 2027-12-31, family rights); rounds 2 and 3 had one reviewer on the delta.

- **Round 1.** No amount, period or date wrong; nothing newer than the texts read. Proven and fixed: the refusal grounds overstated the conviction ground and had unquoted claims; the ticket both ways of Regulations No. 225, point 9, and the means documents' three months (point 4) were missing; the extra check of Regulations No. 554 was missing; the filing deadline in Latvia read as an exact date; the health insurance missed the multiple-entry rule and quoted no Schengen text, so that part left the title; the translation flag was set on some foreign documents only; the Portuguese said "país de origem" for "mītnes zeme" (now "país onde mora"). The claim that the family gets no visa on this ground was tested and stood.
- **Round 2.** Proven and fixed: the lodging cost read the wrong way round and the means missed the multiple-entry rule (point 10); the extra check applies, for three territories, only to people born or living there; the route title put "another" OECD state on the self-employed person too; the conviction ground's refusing clause was not quoted; the ticket had no translation flag; `travel_ticket` was not listed as a new kind.
- **Round 3.** The title, the means, the filing, the refusal grounds, the ticket and the extra check held. Proven: the extra check also reaches people granted stateless or international protection status abroad (Immigration Law, art. 9(1)), and a multiple-entry visa also needs a statement on the means for later stays (Regulations No. 225, point 10). Both fixed after the round (leftover 9).

The proposal check and the app's quote check (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-lv-remote-work`, in `apps/api`) passed in the session after each round.

## Leftover

1. **OECD membership is an assessment.** The law asks for an employer registered in another OECD member state; no country set holds the OECD's members and a session review cannot add one, so the requirement reads only "seated outside Latvia". An employee of a company registered outside the OECD, such as a Brazilian one, reads `needs_assessment`, not `not_eligible`, and the assessment says why.
2. **Where a self-employed person is registered** is not recorded, so a self-employed person, contractor or business owner is always possible, never certain.
3. **The pay threshold has no figure.** Latvia's average monthly gross wage comes from the Central Statistical Bureau, not on the `lv` list; the assessment states the rule (2.5 times the previous year's average) without an amount.
4. **Deadlines in working days** are not durations: the decision in three working days when filed in Latvia less than three working days before the lawful stay ends (Immigration Law, art. 20), and the filing deadline of point 26, whose force-majeure exception the title leaves out.
5. **Not in the version:** the fee exemptions of Regulations No. 731, point 8 (such as a Latvian or Liv, or a child of a Latvian citizen); the notarised parental consent for an applicant under 18 (Regulations No. 676, point 8); interviews (point 6); the right to work for Latvian employers, which this visa does not give.
6. **No apostille or legalisation rule** was found in the texts read, so `apostille` is not set. Art. 10(4) of the Official Language Law lets institutions accept some documents received from abroad without a translation; the review reads it as documents institutions receive, not applicants' papers.
7. **Family.** The route has no dependents; a family member who wants to come needs a ground of their own (a Brazilian may stay visa-free for short stays).
8. **Dates to watch.** Regulations No. 676, 225, 554, 591 and 731 lose force by 1 December 2027 at the latest; new regulations under the new law are a new version from their date. A 2027 minimum wage changes the means.
9. **Corrections no adversarial round saw:** the two fixes after round 3 (the `extra-check` and `subsistence-means` titles, their quotes and Portuguese) and anything the repository's reviewers ask for; the session, the proposal check and the quote check read them.
10. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration and fails if the database already holds this route.
