# Session review 2026-11-au

Australia has one stored route, `au-skilled-independent`: the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189), points-tested stream, a permanent visa for a skilled worker the Minister invites to apply. Its newest version, 2, effective 2026-07-01, was read against the Migration Regulations 1994 (compilation of 2026-11-03), the Migration Act 1958 (compilation of 2026-10-08) and the fee instrument F2026L00874 on the Federal Register of Legislation on 2026-11-20. Version 3 corrects version 2 from the same date ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)).

## What version 3 changes

Version 2 matched the law where it spoke: the age of 45 at invitation, the invitation, the skills assessment, competent English, the points score, the family unit of reg. 1.12(2), the 12 months of a de facto relationship, every first-instalment charge (AUD 6,135, 3,070 and 1,540; Pacific-regional 5,030, 2,515 and 1,260), the second instalment of AUD 4,885 and the 5 years of travel. Version 3 keeps the requirement, every amount and the duration, and changes what version 2 left out or said loosely.

- **The public interest criteria, as the law lists them.** Version 2's `character` item named criteria 4001 to 4004 for the applicant and each family member. Clause 189.211(1) asks of the applicant 4001, 4002, 4003, 4003B, 4004, 4020 and 4021; (2) adds 4019 from 18; (3) asks of each family member applying 4001 to 4004, 4003B, 4010 and 4020, and (9) asks 4001 to 4004 and 4003B of a family member not applying. The secondary criteria (clauses 189.312 and 189.313) add 4021 for each family member applying, 4019 from 18, 4017 and 4018 under 18, 4005 and 4010, and the special return criteria. These are now one assessment each, with the people each covers: `character` (4001, with the police statement and form 80 of reg. 2.03AA), `security` (4002, 4003, 4003B), `commonwealth-debts` (4004), `no-false-information` (4020), `passport` (4021), `values-statement` (4019), `health` (4005, clause 189.225), `establishment` (4010), `minors` (4015 to 4018) and `special-return` (5001, 5002, 5010; clauses 189.212 and 189.226).
- **Other new assessments:** `registered-course` (clause 189.222(2)), `regional-provisional-visa` (3 years on a subclass 491 or 494 visa, clause 189.224A), `where-to-apply` (item 1137(2) and (4A)), `section-48` (a person in Australia without a substantive visa after a refusal or some cancellations may apply only for the classes of reg. 2.12, which do not include Class SI; Act s. 48) and `first-entry` (clause 189.611 and condition 8515).
- **Titles made exact.** The skills assessment's validity (clause 189.222(1)(c) and (d)); the children rules name the exclusion of a child who is engaged (reg. 1.12(2)(b)); the grandchildren rules follow the definition of a dependent child in reg. 1.03 (under 18, or dependent, or unable to work), not only dependence; the spouse rule states Act s. 5F.
- **A family rule tightened.** A child of the spouse or partner aged 23 or more is possible only when recorded as depending on the principal or on the spouse or partner (reg. 1.12(2)(b)(iii)); one depending on no one is no longer a family member. Two cases show both.
- **A source the app can read for the amounts.** The register's HTML writes "$6 135" with a non-breaking space in its own span, which the app's reader takes as two numbers; the fee instrument is now read through its PDF, which states each amount whole.
- **Sources split and re-read.** Schedules 4, 5 and 8 are in the compilation's document 3 and are cited there; every source was read on 2026-11-20.
- **Results.** No result of version 2's cases changes, since version 2 already had assessments.

Nothing published up to 2026-11-20 changes a provision the route cites. The register lists three amendments not yet compiled: F2026L01449 (from 2026-11-12, training visas), F2026L01500 (from 2026-11-13, the Temporary Graduate visa: item 1229 and Part 485) and F2026L01503 (from 2026-11-28, visitor visas: Parts 600, 601 and 651). None touches item 1137, Part 189, Schedules 4 and 5 or the regulations cited. The Migration Act's compilation of 2026-10-08 has no amendment waiting.

## Rounds

Three adversarial rounds, the limit. Two reviewers in rounds 1 and 2: one read each item, title, case and quote against its provision; the other looked for anything official that makes the route out of date or incomplete (the register's versions of the regulations up to 2026-11-28, the amending instruments, the Migration Act and the Marriage Act). Round 3 was one reviewer on the changes.

- **Round 1**, on the first files. No amount, age or date wrong. Proven: the children of the spouse or partner aged 23 or more were possible even when recorded as depending on no one; the engagement exclusion and the grandchildren's dependent-child definition were missing from the titles; the titles of `character`, `security`, `no-false-information`, `special-return` and `skills-assessment` said more or less than their criteria; the section 48 bar and the validity of a spouse's marriage (Act ss. 5F and 12) were missing. Two cases were added.
- **Round 2**, on the corrected files. Proven: the section 48 title counted refusals of bridging visas and on character grounds, which the Act leaves out; the waiver in `no-false-information` reaches only some of criterion 4020, and its under-18 exceptions were missing; `special-return` and `security` stated their waivers and revocations loosely; the spouse title did not name the Marriage Act's Part VA.
- **Round 3**, on those changes. The section 48, security, under-18 and spouse titles and the new sources correct. Proven and fixed after the round (leftover 12): `special-return` read as if a revocation or any later permanent visa cleared criterion 5001, and named any removal, where criterion 5002 names removals under ss. 198, 199 and 205; `no-false-information` no longer said which parts of criterion 4020 the Minister may waive.

The proposal check and the quote check, which reads the official pages through the app's reader (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-au`), pass on the final files.

## Leftover

1. **Age at invitation.** The law reads the age of 45 at the invitation (item 1137(4B)); the version reads it on the evaluation date, so someone invited at 44 who has turned 45 shows as not eligible ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).
2. **Facts no rule can read.** An engagement to be married (reg. 1.12(2)(b)) is not recorded, so an engaged child under 23 reads as a family member. A principal's own child of 23 or more who depends only on the spouse or partner, or is unable to work, reads as possible: the rule on the child's path cannot read dependence on the spouse. A grandchild reads as possible, since a rule cannot test the child in between.
3. **The spouse.** The condition is true: a marriage the case records is read as valid and genuine. The case cannot show a concurrent marriage or a foreign marriage while a party is under 16 (Marriage Act 1961, s. 88D), which the Act does not recognise.
4. **Family violence.** Clauses 189.211(5) and (6) and 189.311(3) to (5), and the nil second instalment of item 1137(4) item 1A, are not modeled: the case does not record it.
5. **The Pacific-regional rate** reads citizenship; the law reads a valid passport issued by a Pacific-regional country (item 1137(3)(a)(i)).
6. **Stand-in facts.** Every assessment shows a stand-in fact (such as `principal.visaRefusals` or `unit.liquidAssets`), since the case records no invitation, skills assessment, visa history or medical result; every person sees every assessment.
7. **Section 48 in detail.** The title names the bar; its continuous-presence rules (s. 48(1B), (2) and (3)), the withdrawal rule (s. 49(3)) and the separate character bar of s. 501E are not in the version.
8. **Not in the version:** the second instalment's deadline of 28 or 70 days after notice (reg. 2.12D); the approved form and the place and manner of applying set by instrument (item 1137(1) and (4A)(a)); the pass mark and the skilled occupation list, set by instrument; the health instrument F2026L01315 that lets some applications skip a Medical Officer (reg. 2.25A); the waivers of criterion 4020(4); the New Zealand and Hong Kong streams.
9. **Dates to watch.** From 2026-11-28 (F2026L01503) a visitor visa granted on an application made from that date carries condition 8503, which bars the grant of a substantive visa such as this one in Australia unless waived (reg. 2.05(4AA)); this changes no item, since applying from outside Australia stays open. The charges are indexed each 1 July: a new amount is a new version.
10. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration.
11. **Versions 1 and 2** hold the same date as version 3 and were not reviewed; an evaluation today selects version 3.
12. **Corrections no adversarial reviewer saw.** After round 3 the titles of `special-return` and `no-false-information` changed as above, and `special-return` gained a quote on the sections of a removal. The session, the proposal check and the quote check read them, and so did the repository's reviewers.
