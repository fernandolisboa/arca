# Session review 2026-11-jp

Japan has one stored route, `jp-nikkei-long-term-resident`: long-term resident status (teijusha) for the third generation of Japanese descent (sansei), with spouse and children, under Ministry of Justice Notice No. 132 of 1990. Its newest version, 2, effective 2022-04-01, was read on 2026-11-20 against the notice (whose page still names Notice No. 220 of 28 October 2021 as the latest amendment), the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act, the Civil Code, the Act on the Calculation of Age, the Enforcement Ordinance and the Enforcement Order on e-Gov, and the Immigration Services Agency's pages on moj.go.jp ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)).

Version 2 matched the law where it spoke: items 3 and 4 of the notice, the spouse of item 5(c), the minor unmarried biological children of item 6(c), the adopted children under 6 of item 7, majority at 18, the 29 February reading, art. 2(i) and art. 5 of the act and the periods of stay. It stays for dates before 2026-10-01. Two new versions follow it.

## Version 3, from 2026-10-01

- **The fee to change status or extend a stay.** Cabinet Order No. 268 of 2026 rewrote art. 25(1)(i) of the Enforcement Order from 2026-10-01: the fee now depends on the period granted (at a counter JPY 18,000 for 6 months, 33,000 for 1 year, 64,000 for 3 years, 75,000 for 5 years; less online), where it was JPY 6,000. Two possible costs, for the principal and for each family member, show the 5-year amount and name the others; they apply only when someone changes to or extends this status. The amounts are quoted from the agency's leaflet, which writes them in figures; the Order writes them in kanji (七万五千円), which the quote check does not read.
- **New assessments.** `landing-documents` and `extension-documents`: the papers of the Enforcement Ordinance's appended tables III and III-7 (family status documents, proof of means or of income and tax, a guarantor living in Japan). `extension-conditions` and `extension-obligations`: what the agency's guideline on changes and extensions weighs (last revised June 2026): still fitting the notice, though an age or support that lapses after entry does not alone bar an extension; good conduct; a self-supporting household; no long absence abroad without good reason; work on terms that meet labour law; taxes paid, and statutory payments such as national health insurance (serious arrears count against); residence card and other notices filed.
- **Titles.** The requirement names both lines of the notice; `family-conditions` says the supporter, and for the spouse's child also the spouse, holds a stay of a year or more; the spouse is a lawful spouse; the duration no longer says "renewable", which its table does not.
- **The same rules, written for the quote check.** The descent rule picked the grandparent with `lineage.generation` equal to 2 (or 3), values no official text writes; it now reads "more than one generation up" within two (or three) generations, which selects the same ancestors in the first line and, in the second, can also reach a grandparent only when the first line already holds for them, so no result changes. A new case shows a child of a Japanese national with no Japanese grandparent is not eligible.
- **The quote check reads 60 months as 5 years.** The ordinance writes the longest period as 五年 and a duration range uses one unit (`P6M` to `P60M`); a count of months that makes whole years now counts as stated by those years ([ADR-0027 amendment](../../../../docs/adr/0027-admins-and-the-checked-batch-activation.md#amendment-2026-11-20-a-count-of-months-stated-as-whole-years)).
- The ordinance's tables III and III-7 are cited through the revision in force from 2026-10-01, so the quotes keep their page after the next revision.

## Version 4, from 2027-03-19

Ordinance No. 43 of 2025 rewrites the long-term resident rows of tables III and III-7 from 2027-03-19: proof of means includes yearly income; tax and social insurance enrolment and payment records are added, also for whoever pays the costs or supports the applicant. Version 4 is version 3 with those two titles and its source on the revision in force from 2027-03-19. Ordinance No. 45 of 2025 (2027-04-01) changes only cross-references, and the Enforcement Order's revision of 2027-04-01 keeps the fees.

## Rounds

Two reviewers in rounds 1 and 2, one checking each item against its provision, the other looking for anything official that makes the route out of date; one reviewer in round 3.

- **Round 1**, on version 3's first files. Proven: the documents change on 2027-03-19 (now version 4); the extension's papers (table III-7) were missing. Raised and settled: the fee also applies to a change of status (now in the titles); the source titles and "renewable" said more than their texts; `family-conditions` read as if only the spouse supports the spouse's children while the rules accept either.
- **Round 2**, on versions 3 and 4. Proven: `family-conditions` had to say the spouse also holds the stay for the spouse's child; `extension-conditions` named factors no quote stated and left out long absences and notices (now split into two assessments with quotes for each); the documents titles left out a change of status.
- **Round 3**, on those changes: no defect. One wording raised and applied after the round (leftover 8): taxes count against when unpaid at all, while the guideline's "serious arrears" qualifies statutory payments such as health insurance.

The proposal check and the quote check, which reads the official pages through the app's reader (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-jp`), pass on the final files.

## Leftover

1. **Family register (honseki), minority and marriage.** As in [OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md): item 4 needs a grandparent who once had a family register in Japan, which the case does not record, so that line reads "missing information", even when the grandparent is recorded as never Japanese; "minor" reads the Japanese Civil Code (18); a same-sex or otherwise unlawful marriage is listed; a principal's own child supported only by the spouse is left out.
2. **"Supported by these persons".** Items 6(c) and 7 say a child of "these persons" supported by "these persons"; the versions read it as a child of the principal or the spouse supported by either of them. A narrower reading (only the child's own parent supports) would drop a spouse's child the principal alone supports; no official text settles it.
3. **The amount shown.** A cost holds one amount: the fee for a 5-year period, the highest; the title names the others. A family extends many times, each time paying by the period granted.
4. **Not in the versions:** the visa fee at a Japanese consulate (mofa.go.jp is not on the `jp` official-domain list, which only the app can extend); the fee for a combined residence and My Number card, optional (Order art. 25(5)); the documents ISA's own page lists for a sansei applicant beyond the ordinance; the second generation (spouse or child of a Japanese national) and the fourth-generation programme, which would be new routes.
5. **Code work.** The quote check does not read kanji amounts (七万五千円: 万 and 円 are not counters it knows) or full-width digits (１８，０００円 on the agency's HTML page), so the Order and the extension page could not evidence the fees.
6. **Watch.** A public comment of 2026-10-29 would add a question to the arrival record (Ordinance form 6); the electronic travel authorization from 2028-10-31 concerns visa-exempt short stays only.
7. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration.
8. **A correction no adversarial reviewer saw.** After round 3 the title of `extension-obligations` split "taxes paid" from the statutory payments that "serious arrears" qualifies. The session, the proposal check and the quote check read it, and so did the repository's reviewers.
