# Session review 2026-11-pt-d2

A session review that adds a route ([ADR-0032, amendment 2026-11-25](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md#amendment-2026-11-25-new-routes), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)). Portugal held two routes, `pt-d7` and `pt-d8`. This review writes `pt-d2` version 1, from 2026-01-01 (the date the EUR 920 minimum wage its means assessment states took effect, Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025), on the `pt` official-domain list both stored routes pin, with the `eu-free-movement` country set at number 2. The sources written for this route were read in full on `diariodarepublica.pt` and its gazette PDFs on 2026-11-25, São Paulo date; those shared with `pt-d8` version 8 keep the dates that review gave them, and their quotes were read again on their pages the same day by the app's quote check.

## The route

The residence visa for independent professional activity or for immigrant entrepreneurs (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 60.º; Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, article 31.º). Four alternative paths: a contract or written proposal for services in a liberal profession (or a company contract, by the regulation), with the qualification to practise where the profession needs one; investment operations already made; financial means available in Portugal with a described intent to invest; or an entrepreneurial project integrated in a certified incubator (the Startup Visa programme, Portaria n.º 344/2017 and Despacho Normativo n.º 4/2018).

- **Which text is in force.** Article 60.º reads as the Lei n.º 18/2022 republication, with n.º 2, c), as Decreto-Lei n.º 41/2023 reworded it; Lei n.º 61/2025 and Lei n.º 62/2026 leave it alone. Article 31.º of the regulation reads as the Decreto Regulamentar n.º 4/2022 republication (n.º 2, a), and n.º 3 revoked, d) added, by Decreto Regulamentar n.º 9/2018); Decreto Regulamentar n.º 1/2024 does not touch it. The gazette site's consolidated pages show stale wording for both articles (article 60.º without n.º 2, c); article 31.º with the revoked n.º 2, a), and n.º 3), so the sources cite the gazette PDFs.
- **Requirement the case decides.** Not a citizen of an EU or EEA state or of Switzerland, as in `pt-d8`.
- **Means are an assessment, not a requirement.** Portaria n.º 1563/2007 sets the scale (article 2.º, n.º 2) and the period (article 5.º: for independent work the visa's stay with its extension, measured by availability in Portugal "designadamente" through the services or company contract of article 31.º, n.º 1, a); at least 12 months for an investor). A person with no income or savings today can meet it with the contract the case cannot record, so no amount can make the route "not eligible". The assessment states the scale's amounts.
- **Assessments.** Which path applies and its proof; the IRS list of professions and the qualification (independent path); the means scale and period; the Startup Visa conditions for the entrepreneur, the project, its potential and a startup already set up in the home country (incubator path); entry ban, conviction, public order and an earlier illegal stay (article 52.º, n.os 1, 3, 4 and 10).
- **Family, fees, durations and general documents** are `pt-d8` version 8's, which rest on the same articles (58.º, n.º 5, and 99.º of the law; 12.º and 24.º-B of the regulation; the consular fee table).
- **Documents of this visa** (article 31.º): the company or services contract or proposal and the statement on special qualifications (independent path); proof of investment operations, proof of means in Portugal with the described intent, or IAPMEI's incubation statement (entrepreneur path). Each "may apply": no case fact tells the path. Kinds reuse `service_contract` and `investment_evidence`; new kinds `professional_practice_statement`, `investment_plan` and `incubation_contract_statement` are issued in Portugal, so Brazil's document guide gets no entry.

## Rounds

Two adversarial reviewers, one reading every item against the downloaded provisions, one searching the gazette for anything that makes the route out of date or incomplete.

- **Round 1.** Fixed: the means period claimed Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 5.º, n.º 6's 12 months for every visa (it opens "sem prejuízo" and names the proofs of retirees and people living on asset income), now dropped; the list of professions and the qualification now apply only to the independent path; the means scale now gives dependent adult children 30%, as article 2.º, n.º 2, c) does (the title copied from `pt-d8` gave every other adult 50%); "a certified incubator" now says "a project in a certified incubator". Added: the Startup Visa rules, Portaria n.º 344/2017 and Despacho Normativo n.º 4/2018, which the first draft had not found, and the public order and illegal stay refusal grounds (article 52.º, n.os 4 and 10). Kept as leftover: findings on the AIMA and UCFE prior opinion, the residence permit's conditions and the pre-authorization (5 to 7), and the adult child who depends only on the principal's spouse (8).
- **Round 2.** Fixed: the project assessment stopped at article 5.º, n.º 2, e), and nothing covered n.º 3, for a startup set up in the home country; both now have their own assessment. "Taxes and social security in order" now carries the despacho's "where applicable". Both reviewers confirmed the despacho and the portaria are in force and unamended after Portaria n.º 275/2018, and that the gazette has no act from 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-25 on this visa.
- **Round 3** read the four changed Startup Visa items and found them right in substance, with four low wording gaps, fixed after it and seen by no adversarial reviewer (leftover 11): the project is to be developed in Portugal; "able to form a company" takes "where applicable"; and the startup set up in the home country must operate in Portugal, create at least 5 jobs besides the founders, and its founder may hold 10% of the votes instead of the capital.

The app's quote check (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-pt-d2`, in `apps/api`) passed in the session after each round. The `review-quotes` job runs it again on the pull request.

## Leftover

1. **The consulates' own list is not read.** `vistos.mne.gov.pt` and `aima.gov.pt` refuse this session and are not on Portugal's official-domain list. What a consulate asks beyond the law and the regulation (a business plan, a Portuguese tax number or bank account, proof of accommodation) is not listed, and neither is IAPMEI's Startup Visa platform.
2. **No requirement decides the paths.** The law sets no minimum investment, and neither past work nor an existing business is a condition, so the work periods and business details the case records (#509, #512) are evidence for the assessments, not requirements. A Startup Visa applicant's 18 years and 12 times the IAS are conditions of one path only, and the 2026 IAS was not read on an official page.
3. **The means period for retirees and asset income.** Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 5.º, n.º 6, asks 12 months of means "sem prejuízo" of the paragraphs before it, proved by a pension or asset income; whether a consulate applies it to a D2 applicant who lives on such income is not settled (the same open point as `pt-d8`).
4. **The Startup Visa's procedure.** Despacho Normativo n.º 4/2018, article 5.º, n.º 4 (the conditions hold for the whole programme), n.º 5 (at most 5 entrepreneurs per application), article 6.º (IAPMEI decides in 30 business days, then 40 business days to sign the incubation contract) and article 7.º, n.º 5 (a 12-month contract, renewable). Business-day periods are not durations, and the steps come before the visa application.
5. **The prior opinion** of AIMA and UCFE (Lei n.º 23/2007, article 53.º, n.os 1, 6 and 9: binding if negative, within 20 days, waivable in urgent cases for this visa) runs inside the 60-day decision time and is not a separate duration.
6. **The residence permit's conditions** (article 89.º: the company set up or activity declared, social security registration, and, from Lei n.º 62/2026, a change of activity by notice to AIMA) belong to the stage after arrival, as in `pt-d7` and `pt-d8`; only the permit's two-year duration is stated.
7. **The pre-authorization** of article 58.º, n.º 6 (provisional tax, social security and health numbers with the visa) is information, not a condition.
8. **An adult child of the principal alone who depends only on the principal's spouse is left out.** Article 99.º, n.º 1, d), says "a cargo do casal ou de um dos cônjuges"; no fact reads a candidate's dependence on the spouse when the child is not the spouse's, so the child rule reads dependence on the principal, as `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` do (their route notes list it). A child of both reads correctly through the spouse's path.
9. **The CPLP residence visa and statement of responsibility.** A national of a state where the CPLP mobility agreement is in force, Brazil included, may file a CPLP residence visa instead (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, articles 12.º, n.º 3, and 24.º-A), a different visa no stored route models, and may replace proof of means with a statement of responsibility (article 12.º-A, n.º 6), which the case does not record; as leftover 3 of the [2026-11-pt-documents review](../2026-11-pt-documents/README.md#leftover).
10. **The 2027 minimum wage** ([#220](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/220)) is not decreed yet; when it is, `pt-d2` version 2 from 2027-01-01 must follow `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` version 9, keeping version 1's documents.
11. **Corrections after the third round**, which no adversarial reviewer saw: the four wording points of round 3 in `startup-visa-project`, `startup-visa-project-potential` and `startup-visa-existing-startup`, in English and Portuguese, checked only by the session, the tests and the repository's reviewers.
12. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration and fails if the database already holds this route.
