# Session review 2026-11-lv

Latvia has one stored route, `lv-capital-investment`: a temporary residence permit for an investment in a company's share capital (Immigration Law, art. 27(1) point 10). Its newest version, 4, effective 2026-09-15, was read against the official texts on likumi.lv on 2026-11-20. Version 5 corrects version 4 from the same date ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)).

## What version 5 changes

Version 4 matched the law where it spoke: EUR 50,000 or EUR 100,000 invested and EUR 10,000 paid to the state budget, the company's size and yearly taxes, the limit of ten investors, the exclusion of Russian, Belarusian, EU, EEA and Swiss citizens, the family rules, the fee of EUR 160 and the 30-day answer. Version 5 keeps every expression and amount. It adds what version 4 left out: the general conditions every temporary residence permit must meet. The law keeps them in its art. 34, and in the earlier Cabinet regulations that transitional provision 3 keeps in force, as far as they do not contradict the law, until new ones replace them and no later than 1 December 2027.

- **Sixteen new assessments**, one provision each:
  - `new-capital`: the investment increases a company's share capital or founds a new company (art. 27(1) point 10).
  - `company-tax-debt`: the company has no registered tax debt above EUR 150 (art. 34(1) point 26).
  - `company-taxes-first-year`: the earlier regulations ask, for an incomplete first reporting year, monthly taxes averaging EUR 3,300 or EUR 8,300 (Regulations No. 564, points 65 and 65¹). The new law dropped that clause, which the old one had in its art. 23(7¹) to (7⁴), so the title says so (leftover 5).
  - `call`: a request needs a call (izsaukums) approved by the Office, unless the applicant is the sole officer of a registered company with the right to represent it (art. 25(1); Regulations No. 564, point 7).
  - `subsistence-means`: the minimum monthly wage a month, EUR 780 in 2026, for each person who asks for a permit and 30 per cent of it for a child, each request judged on its own (art. 34(1) point 4; Regulations No. 225, points 3, 11.7 and 12; Regulations No. 656, point 2).
  - `health-insurance` and `health-insurance-cover`: a valid policy shown on receiving the permit, with a limit of at least EUR 42,600, for the whole expected stay, covering emergency help, critical in-patient care and transport (Regulations No. 564, point 73.2; No. 591, points 6, 7.2 and 9).
  - `request-documents` and `police-certificate`: what is handed in with the request; the police certificate is asked of those over 14 and not of someone who needs no visa to enter Latvia (Regulations No. 564, points 28 and 29).
  - `family-documents` and `child-travel-consent`: the marriage document, the kinship documents, a court ruling on guardianship or curatorship, taken over by a Latvian orphans' court when foreign, and the consent of a parent who does not come, notarised or signed before an official, or an authority's document when the parent who comes has daily care (Regulations No. 564, points 39 to 41).
  - `no-criminal-conviction`, `no-entry-ban` and `no-active-tuberculosis`: three refusal grounds (art. 34(1) points 8, 7 and 5; Regulations No. 825, point 2; No. 564, point 73.3).
  - `where-to-file`: where the documents may be filed (art. 32(1)).
  - `collect-within-three-months`: the decision is valid for three months from the day it is taken and is not extended (Regulations No. 564, points 72 and 75).
- **Five new costs.** For the call, all possible since they are owed only when a call is needed: EUR 10 that the inviter pays for the request on the state portal's form, EUR 17 another way, and EUR 10 for each person the request includes, the principal and each family member (Regulations No. 731, points 3 and 7.3). For the permit card, certain: EUR 45 for each person in 10 working days, EUR 80 in two (Regulations No. 133 of 2012, point 4²).
- **The permit's term is no longer a duration.** The law writes it in words ("līdz diviem gadiem"), and the quote check reads no number written in Latvian words, so no official passage can prove a duration of two years. The term is now in the route's title (leftover 1).
- **Titles made exact.** `company-size` counts the larger company's employees with its Latvian subsidiaries; `ten-investors` says the law puts the limit under the EUR 50,000 tier; the answer may take up to four months when objective reasons prevent 30 days (art. 33(4)); the payment to the state budget is made for a first permit before receiving it and refunded only in the Cabinet's cases (art. 30; Regulations No. 564, points 71, 73¹ and 73²); a late renewal or a new investment object pays as a first permit (art. 29(3)); a child of 18 or more is possible when under curatorship or, on renewal, when a minor at the first request (art. 29(2)).
- **The age of 18 has a source in figures.** The Civil Law writes it in words; the Law on the Protection of the Rights of the Child, art. 3(1), says the same in figures with the same exceptions, and is cited beside it.
- **Four new cases:** a child the day before and on the 18th birthday, a Swiss principal (not eligible) and a Russian spouse of a Brazilian principal (a dependent, since art. 27(8) bars Russian and Belarusian citizens only from the investor's own permit).
- **Results.** No result changes: every case of version 4 reads the same, since version 4 already had assessments.
- **Every source was read on 2026-11-20**, and every `verifiedAt` says so.

Nothing published up to 2026-11-20 changes a provision the route cites. The Immigration Law has two redactions, the one in force since 2026-09-15 and one for 2027-12-31, when art. 48 takes effect. No regulations under art. 28 exist, so no nationality is suspended. The one act issued under the new law, Cabinet Regulations No. 669 of 10 November 2026 on entry restrictions (in force 2026-11-20, under art. 8(2)), limits visa applications and entry for citizens of Russia and Belarus and changes no item here. The Civil Law's redaction of 2026-11-11 left arts. 219 to 221 as they were. Regulations No. 133 of 2012 were last amended with effect from 2025-01-01 and are issued under the identity documents law, so the 2027 deadline does not reach them.

## Rounds

Three adversarial rounds, the limit. Two reviewers in rounds 1 and 2: one read each item, title, case and quote against its provision; the other looked for anything official that makes the route out of date or incomplete (the redactions and amending acts on likumi.lv, the acts issued under the new law, the old law of 2002 that the earlier regulations were written under). Round 3 was one reviewer on the delta.

- **Round 1**, on the first files. No amount, age or date wrong. Proven: the call fee on the portal is EUR 10, which the regulations make the usual way; the permit card fee of EUR 45 was missing; the refund cases, art. 29(2) for children of 18 or more, the first-year tax averages, the family documents and the police certificate were missing or loose; the insurance was one item for several provisions; several titles said more or less than their provision (ten investors, company taxes, where to file, the three-month decision, the Civil Law's art. 220). Four cases were added.
- **Round 2**, on the corrected files. Every quote on its page. Proven: the payment's title put it at the request, while the regulations put it after the decision to grant; the first-year averages rest on a clause the new law dropped; the family documents missed point 41 and the other ways to give a parent's consent; the call fee's title no longer said the inviter pays; the card fee's point is 4², not 4.2; one quote ran past the sentence it was for.
- **Round 3**, on that delta. The decision's validity, the first-year averages, the call fee, the family documents and the source titles correct. Proven, and fixed after the round (leftover 12): a refund needs a written request within the four months, not only a permit not received; the authority's document replaces the parent's consent only when no limit on moving the child is set; the quote for the insurance's term stopped before the words "the stay in Latvia".

The proposal check and the quote check, which reads the official pages through the app's reader (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-lv`), pass on the final files.

## Leftover

1. **The permit's term is not a duration.** The quote check reads number words only in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Italian (`packages/domain/src/quote-check/number-words.ts`), and the law writes "līdz diviem gadiem". The term is in the title; the fix is Latvian number words in the quote check, then a duration in a later version ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).
2. **Minors the version cannot tell apart.** A minor the orphans' court declared of age (Civil Law, art. 220) reads as a minor, since the case does not record it. One who married reads as of age, as art. 221 says.
3. **Dependents the version cannot reach.** A ward or a person under curatorship who is not recorded as a child, such as a parent, is not a dependent, since no fact records curatorship.
4. **The capital requirement is a stand-in.** It reads liquid assets of EUR 60,000 for an investment already made, and only the EUR 50,000 tier; the larger tier and the company's size are assessments.
5. **The first-year tax averages.** The earlier regulations ask them (points 65 and 65¹), the new law dropped the clause from its text, and transitional provision 3 keeps the regulations only where they do not contradict the law. No official text says which reading the Office follows; the item is an assessment, so no result turns on it.
6. **Call fees.** Whether a family member is in the principal's call request or one of their own changes which fee is owed. Both ways are in the titles, and all call fees are possible.
7. **The assessments show a stand-in fact.** The case records no company, no call, no insurance, no conviction and no filing place, so each assessment names the unit's liquid assets or the principal's citizenships, and every person sees every assessment.
8. **Not in the version:** the right to work (art. 44, points 20 and 23); what happens on the investor's death (art. 36(5)); linked refusals and annulments for family members (arts. 34(3) and 36); the conviction test on renewal (art. 34(1) point 9); holders of permits from before 15 September 2026 (transitional provisions 4 and 6 to 9); filing online without a secure signature and through an authorised person (Regulations No. 564, points 2.3, 2.4 and 3.3); proof of the expected address on receiving the permit (point 73.5); a call's validity of six months (point 11.1); the card made in 10 working days as a duration (the schema counts calendar days); the long-stay visa a visa-required national may need to enter.
9. **Dates to watch.** Regulations No. 564, 225, 591 and 825 lose force by 1 December 2027 and No. 731 on 2 December 2027 at the latest; new regulations under the new law are a new version from their date. A minimum monthly wage for 2027 (Regulations No. 656) changes the means of subsistence; none was published by 2026-11-20.
10. **Family members of EU citizens.** Whether a Brazilian married to an EU citizen falls under the EU regime instead was not checked for Latvia ([OPEN-QUESTIONS](../../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).
11. **The state-fund permit** (art. 27(1) point 36, EUR 150,000) is another route; a review cannot add one, so it goes through a new-route request in the app.
12. **Corrections no adversarial reviewer saw.** After round 3 the titles of `state-budget-payment` and `child-travel-consent` gained the two conditions above, and `health-insurance` one more quote. The session, the proposal check and the quote check read them, and so did the repository's reviewers.
13. **Versions 1 to 3** hold the same dates as version 4 and were not reviewed; an evaluation today selects version 5.
14. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration.
