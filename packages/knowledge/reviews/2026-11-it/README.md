# Session review 2026-11-it

Italy has one stored route, `it-descent` (recognition of Italian citizenship by descent, for someone born abroad). Its two newest versions were read against the texts on `normattiva.it` on 2026-11-20: version 4, effective 2026-02-19, and version 5, effective 2029-01-01, when adults living abroad start applying to a central office. Version 6 corrects version 4 and version 7 corrects version 5, each from the same date ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md)).

## What versions 6 and 7 change

- **The exception of art. 3-bis, letter c.** Versions 4 and 5 wanted the parent or grandparent who holds only Italian citizenship to be in the line that hands the citizenship down, link by link. Legge 91/1992, art. 3-bis, comma 1, c), asks only that "un genitore o un nonno possiede, o possedeva al momento della morte, esclusivamente la cittadinanza italiana". The requirement is now two conditions: a parent who was Italian when the person was born (art. 1), and one of the exceptions.
- **No other citizenship.** Art. 3-bis reaches only someone born abroad who "è in possesso di altra cittadinanza". A person recorded with no other citizenship and an Italian parent now meets the requirement; versions 4 and 5 read "Faltam informações".
- **A line never recognized.** Versions 4 and 5 read "not eligible" when the parent is recorded with no Italian citizenship though an ancestor above was Italian. By the law of the time the child of a citizen was a citizen by birth unless they lost it (Legge 555/1912, arts. 1, 7, 8 and 12; Legge 91/1992, art. 1), and families record such people with the citizenship they use. A line below an ancestor who was Italian when the next person in the line was born now reads as possible, as `pl-descent` reads it, up to the four generations the schema reaches. So a great-grandchild reads "Faltam informações", not "not eligible": letter c is out of reach, but letter d and a birth in Italy cannot be recorded (leftover 6).
- **The person's own loss.** New assessment `no-own-loss`: someone who lost Italian citizenship themselves is outside this route (Legge 555/1912, art. 8, until 15 August 1992; Legge 91/1992, art. 11, since).
- **A link through a mother before 1948.** New assessment `link-through-a-mother-before-1948`: until the Constitution took effect on 1 January 1948 the law passed citizenship through the father (Legge 555/1912, art. 1; Corte costituzionale, sentenza 30/1983), and the administration accepts a maternal link only for those born after that date (the circular quoted in ordinanza 147/2026). It warns a family whose Italian parent was never recognized (leftover 7).
- **Minors.** New assessment `minor-at-consulate`: a minor living abroad, child of a citizen already recognized, goes to the consulate (and still does from 2029), outside the yearly limit and the EUR 600 fee, which are for adults (Decreto legislativo 71/2011, art. 10, comma 1, b).
- **Court.** New assessments `court-proof` (the claimant states and proves that no cause of non-acquisition or loss applies; no oath, and no witnesses save where the law provides: Decreto legislativo 150/2011, art. 19-bis, commi 2-bis and 2-ter) and `court-venue` (for a claimant living abroad the case follows the municipality where the Italian parent or ancestor was born: Decreto-legge 13/2017, art. 4, comma 5).
- **Central office, version 7.** New assessment `central-office-notices` (art. 10, comma 4: notices only by e-mail, and the originals of a rejected application come back at the applicant's cost). The duration's title now covers the central office and the applications consulates received before 2029 (comma 7).
- **One fee, not three.** Versions 4 and 5 had three possible costs of EUR 600 (consulate or central office, municipality, court). A person pays one of them, and the engine adds possible costs up, so the possible total read EUR 1,800. They are now one possible cost of EUR 600 whose title names the three paths. The court fee is due for each claimant of any age (D.P.R. 115/2002, art. 13, comma 1-sexies); the other two are for adults.
- **Route title.** It now leaves out who filed an application or a court claim, or was given an appointment, by 27 March 2025 (art. 3-bis, letters a, a-bis and b), not only who filed an application.
- **Sources.** The consular fee table is cited at the address of the annex itself, which the app can read, and its note 12 quotes comma 639 of Legge 207/2024, so the separate source for that comma is gone. Legge 555/1912 is cited at its text in force until 15 August 1992. The Constitutional Court's pages are now `secondary` (leftover 3). Every title was checked against its provision, and every `verifiedAt` is 2026-11-20 except ordinanza 147/2026, whose page did not answer and whose text was read in the Court's open data.

The parts of versions 4 and 5 that matched their sources stay: the consulate's yearly limit until 31 December 2028 with its floor of 100, the central office by post from 1 January 2029 with its limit in 2029 and 2030, the time limit of 36 months, and the amounts of EUR 600.

Nothing published up to 2026-11-20 changes a provision the route cites. Art. 3-bis is unchanged since 24 May 2025. The last amendment to Legge 91/1992 is Legge 199/2025, art. 1, comma 513, which touches art. 4, comma 1-bis, and art. 9-bis, on other ways to citizenship (leftover 5). Art. 10 of Decreto legislativo 71/2011 is the text Legge 11/2026 wrote. The Constitutional Court ruled on art. 3-bis in sentenza 63/2026 (challenges unfounded or inadmissible) and asked the EU Court of Justice about it in ordinanza 147/2026; no later ruling of the Court, up to n. 206 of 13 November 2026, decides on it, and five more referrals wait (registro ordinanze 4, 40, 41, 172 and 176 of 2026).

## Rounds

Three adversarial rounds, the limit, by two reviewers: one read each item, title, case and quote against its provision; the other looked for anything official that makes the route out of date or incomplete (each cited act's update list on Normattiva, the Gazzetta Ufficiale of 2026, the Constitutional Court's rulings and pending referrals of 2026). Round 1, on the first files: nothing out of date and no wrong date or amount; the requirement tied letter c to the line of descent, failed a person with no other citizenship, read an adoptive parent without testing citizenship and ignored the person's own loss; the route title ignored letter a-bis; the fee title called the court fee an adult's; the court venue, the central office's notices and the minors at consulates were missing. Round 2, on the corrected files: nothing out of date; every quote is in its page and supports its item; one regression, a parent born before 1948 whose own link was no longer tested, answered with the assessment `link-through-a-mother-before-1948`; one source title and the route title made exact. Round 3, on that delta: all three changes correct, and every case gives its expected result. Only the second reviewer's round-2 notes on completeness were not sent back to a reviewer; they are leftovers 2 and 14 and the route title's "or court claim", which round 3 read. The proposal check and the quote check, which reads the official pages through the app's reader (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-it`), pass on the final files.

## Leftover

1. **The municipal fee has no quote.** Legge 207/2024, art. 1, comma 636 (up to EUR 600 for each adult applicant at a municipality that chooses to charge) was read in this session, but Normattiva serves commi 601 to 700 only to a reader that keeps its session cookie, which the app's reader does not. The cost's quotes state EUR 600 from the consular table and the court costs act; the municipal leg rests on the session's reading.
2. **Old civil status certificates.** Comma 637 of the same article lets a municipality charge up to EUR 300 for a certificate or extract of a civil status record more than a century old. It is not a cost of the route, for the same reason as leftover 1 and because no fact records how many certificates a family needs.
3. **The Constitutional Court's site refuses automated readers** after a few requests (a redirect to a bot challenge), and production failed on it on 2026-11-14. Its three rulings are cited as `secondary`, so no item depends on reading them; sentenze 30/1983 and 63/2026 were read on the site on 2026-11-20, and ordinanza 147/2026 in the Court's open data (`dati.cortecostituzionale.it`).
4. **`cortedicassazione.it` is not on the `it` official-domain list**, and a session review cannot add a host: that is a new list version, decided by an admin in the app. Sezioni Unite 24045/2026 stays `secondary`. It was re-read on 2026-11-20 and changes no expression: it holds that a minor born abroad, a citizen of that state by birth on its soil, kept Italian citizenship when the Italian parent naturalized, which bears on how a family records a link's citizenship, not on the rule.
5. **A minor's declaration is another route.** Legge 91/1992, art. 4, comma 1-bis, lets the parents of a minor who is barred by art. 3-bis declare the wish to acquire citizenship (letter b: within three years of the birth, as Legge 199/2025 now reads), and Decreto-legge 36/2025, art. 1, comma 1-ter, gives until 31 May 2029 for someone who was a minor on 24 May 2025 and whose parent is a citizen under letters a, a-bis or b. That is acquisition by declaration, not recognition by descent. A review cannot add a route; it is a new-route request in the app. The same goes for reacquisition by former citizens (art. 17, from 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2027).
6. **Exceptions the case cannot record read as possible:** letter d (a parent or adoptive parent who lived in Italy for two continuous years after becoming a citizen and before the birth or adoption), birth in Italy (art. 3-bis does not reach it), and applications, appointments or court claims by 27 March 2025 (letters a, a-bis and b). So a person with an Italian parent and another citizenship never reads "not eligible" here: the result is "Faltam informações".
7. **No fact records sex.** The person's own birth before 1 January 1948 reads as possible, whether the Italian parent was the father or the mother. A parent recorded as Italian when the person was born is taken as recorded, and how that parent came to the citizenship is not derived again: when that parent was born before 1948 to an Italian mother and was never recognized, only a court recognizes the link, which the new assessment says and the requirement does not test. Round 2 raised it, and round 3 found that no family is left with a result the provisions contradict: the courts apply the 1983 ruling to births before the Constitution (Cassazione 4466/2009, as ordinanza 147/2026 records), and the assessment states the administration's limit. Its title leaves out the narrow cases in which the 1912 law did pass citizenship through the mother (art. 1, n. 2: father unknown, stateless, or the child not taking his citizenship).
8. **A loss along the line is not recorded** when a person is listed without Italian citizenship: below an ancestor who was Italian when their child was born the line reads as possible, whether those below were never recognized, lost the citizenship as adults, or lost it as minors with their own parent (Legge 555/1912, art. 12). A loss recorded on the ancestor before the child's birth does close the line.
9. **A dead ancestor's citizenships are read as held at death.** The case has no date of death, so letter c's "possedeva al momento della morte" is read from the recorded list.
10. **Adoption.** An adoptive parent who is or was Italian reads as possible: the case records when the adoption began, but not whether it was of a minor, which Legge 91/1992, art. 3, asks.
11. **The assessments show a stand-in fact.** The case does not record the path (consulate, municipality in Italy, court), the person's own past citizenships, or whether a parent is already recognized, so each assessment shows the principal's country of residence or citizenships, and every person sees every assessment.
12. **The fee reads no age.** The consular and municipal fees are for adults and the court fee is for everyone; the cost is possible for all, since an age needs an official source for a 29 February birthday and the path is not recorded.
13. **Pending in the courts.** The EU Court of Justice has not answered ordinanza 147/2026; an answer against art. 3-bis is a new version. A notice of the Court of Cassation in the Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 216 of 17 September 2026 announces the collection of signatures for a referendum to strike from art. 3-bis the words that reach people born before it took effect and the word "esclusivamente" of letter c.
14. **Version 7 is read again before 2029.** The ministerial rules for the central office are not out, and the title of `central-office-by-post` leaves out that the Ministry may use outside operators at the user's cost (art. 10, comma 3). Its limit ends with 2030, and the schema has no end date, so a version from 2031-01-01 must drop the assessment `central-office-yearly-limit`. The consulate's limit ends the same way with version 7.
15. **Versions 1 to 5** were not reviewed beyond versions 4 and 5. Version 1 still holds 2025-05-24 to 2026-02-18, a past period that an evaluation made today does not select.
16. **Corrections no adversarial reviewer saw.** After round 3 the repository's reviewers asked for three changes, checked only by the session, the proposal check and the quote check. The open branch of the requirement was a parent below an Italian grandparent, with tests of the kind of link and of births before 1948 that could never decide it; it failed a person adopted by such a parent and a great-grandchild with a firm "not eligible". It is now any recorded ancestor who was Italian when their child in the line was born, which only turns "not eligible" into "Faltam informações". Two cases were added (an only-Italian grandmother outside the Italian parent's line; an adoptive parent below an Italian grandfather) and the great-grandfather case now expects "Faltam informações". Two titles were made clearer: the requirement's (the person, not the parent, holds no other citizenship) and version 7's fee (the path is not recorded, as version 6's says).
17. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration and fails if the database already holds a version 6 or 7.
