# Session review 2026-11-us-documents

The documents of the United States' one stored route, `us-eb5` ([#524](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/524), spec [#519](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/519)), and the first version of Brazil's document guide ([ADR-0039](../../../../docs/adr/0039-document-guides.md)). Version 10 corrects version 9 from its date, 2026-11-30: it keeps every item of version 9 and adds the route's official document list, `documents` ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md#documents)). Every source the documents cite was read on 2026-11-25, São Paulo date.

## What version 10 adds

**The investor's petition to USCIS** (`principal`): the Form I-526E or I-526 petition (the 2019-11-20 text of 8 CFR 204.6(a), which a court restored); the optional regional center certification that incorporates the offering's records ([8 U.S.C. 1153(b)(5)(E)(ii)(III)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title8/html/USCODE-2024-title8-chap12-subchapII-partI-sec1153.htm)); the signed fee disclosure ((K)(iv)); evidence of the enterprise and its 10 jobs, of the capital placed at risk, of the investor's engagement (the 2019-11-20 text of 8 CFR 204.6(j)(5), which a court restored); and the source-of-funds records the 2022 Act requires ((L)(ii) and (iii)): every source of the capital and fees, foreign business registration records, tax returns of the past 7 years, judgment and litigation records, who transfers the funds, and the donor's or lender's records for gifted or borrowed money. Documents in a foreign language go with a certified English translation (8 CFR 103.2(b)(3)).

**The immigrant visa at a consulate abroad** (`principal` and `each_dependent`): Form DS-260, passport (valid 60 days beyond the visa), birth record, police certificates, any prison or military record, the relationship records for a spouse or an adopted child, photographs and the panel physician's medical examination (8 U.S.C. 1202(b), 22 CFR 42.63 to 42.66). The case does not record whether a family takes its visa at a consulate or adjusts status in the U.S., so these may apply, as the consular fees of version 9 do.

**Vaccinations** (everyone): the documentation 8 U.S.C. 1182(a)(1)(A)(ii) asks of immigrants and of anyone adjusting status, with the waivers of 1182(g)(2) in its note.

Conditions: items the law asks "as applicable", "if any" or "optional" may apply; the petition, the fee disclosure, the enterprise and job evidence, the capital at risk, the source of funds, the engagement and vaccinations apply to everyone they name; spouse records read `person.hasSpouse`; adoption records read the dependent's last edge. No item sets an apostille or a validity: no cited U.S. source states one.

**Brazil's document guide, version 1** (`packages/knowledge/document-guides/1.json`, wording in `pt-BR-document-guides.ts`): passport (Polícia Federal), police certificate (Polícia Federal), birth and marriage certificates (Lei 6.015/1973), tax return copies (Receita Federal), federal court certificates (Justiça Federal's unified system), vaccination record (Meu SUS Digital) and the Banco Central's foreign exchange and transfers report (Registrato, as `fund_transfer_records`), with the CNJ's apostille rules (Provimento 149/2023). Domains: `gov.br` and `jus.br`; Brazil has no official-domain list in the knowledge base, so these are the departure checklist's (ADR-0038).

## Rounds

Three adversarial rounds at most: one reviewer checked every item and guide entry against its cited passage, one looked for missing or out-of-date requirements.

- Round 1: 14 findings on the items and the guide, 11 on gaps. Fixed: the court certificate entry mixed two TRF1 services; judgment records left out private civil actions; the capital-at-risk title dropped the loan's personal liability; the enterprise item claimed a standalone-only scope no source states; four required records were missing (the petition form, the regional center certification, the fee disclosure, the fund transferors); the apostille text cited Provimento 62/2017 art. 3, revoked by Provimento 149/2023; several pt-BR renderings (Hib, shares, the reservist certificate, the police note's six months).
- Round 2: 9 findings on the items and the guide, 4 on gaps. Fixed: the petition item now cites the restored 2019 text of 204.6(a) and the fee rule's form names; the fee disclosure and the enterprise evidence apply to every investor, not "may apply"; the fee disclosure's exception is what the business plan "specifically" identifies; the regional center certification is optional; the enterprise item notes that regional center jobs may be indirect; the police certificate note keeps the consul's "where a police record is believed to exist"; the vaccination note gives all three waivers of 1182(g)(2) with their own source; pt-BR "como devedor principal" and "influenza tipo B (Hib)"; the Registrato entry moved to `fund_transfer_records`, which matches what the report shows; the apostille text no longer calls the interior's service optional; the tax return copies name each channel the Receita's page gives.
- Round 3: no finding on the round 2 fixes' quotes; 2 findings and 1 nit, fixed after the round and checked only by the quote check: the enterprise note now gives the 2022 Act's cap on indirect jobs, 90% or 75% for construction under 2 years (8 U.S.C. 1153(b)(5)(E)(iv)), in place of the regulation's "directly or indirectly" and the regional center's business plan, which the certification item already covers; the vaccination waiver needs a panel physician's or civil surgeon's certificate, not any doctor's; pt-BR "influenza tipo B" without "(Hib)", as the statute reads.

The quote check, the app's own (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-us-documents`, in `apps/api`), passed in the session on 2026-11-25 UTC.

## Leftover

1. **The list shows from 2026-11-30.** Version 10 corrects version 9, which takes effect then; the version in effect until 2026-11-29 has no document list, so the checklist says Arca does not know until then.
2. **Consular or adjustment of status.** No case fact says where the visa is issued, so every consular item may apply. Adjustment of status in the U.S. (Form I-485 and its evidence) is not listed: its sources are on `uscis.gov`, which refuses this session.
3. **Nothing from the State Department.** `travel.state.gov` is not on the route's official-domain list and refuses this session; its document pages (translations of civil documents, police certificate details by country, divorce and death records) are not used. Only a list change in the app can add the host.
4. **Targeted employment area evidence.** 8 CFR 204.6(j)(6), as the 2019-11-20 text restores it, lets a state designate a high unemployment area, which 8 U.S.C. 1153(b)(5)(B)(ii) now forbids; the rural prong alone could be listed, but the item is left out until the final rule after 91 FR 40676 settles both. The regional center carve-outs of the engagement and capital-at-risk evidence follow the restored text as it reads.
5. **The 2026 proposed rule.** 91 FR 40676 would move the evidence to a new subpart, add path-of-funds records and drop the engagement evidence; the list follows the rule when it is final.
6. **A stepchild's own relationship record.** A per-dependent condition cannot read the first edge of the path, so no item asks a stepchild for the marriage that makes the step relationship; the principal's marriage record and the child's birth record are on the lists.
7. **Fingerprints** (22 CFR 42.67(c)) are biometrics taken at the interview, not a paper, and are not listed.
8. **Brazil's guide does not cover** state courts and the labor courts' certificate (CNDT) (`court_records` covers only the Justiça Federal); the Juntas Comerciais' certificates (`business_registration`, one per state); military records (`military_record`, the in-country channel is on `eb.mil.br`, outside the guide's domains); prison records, adoption decrees, photographs, medical examinations, visa forms and the enterprise's own documents, which no Brazilian office issues as such. The Justiça Federal's unified certificate is sourced from the TRF1's 2024 announcement, the newest official page that describes it.
9. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration.
