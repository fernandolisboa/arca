# Session review 2026-11-pt-documents

The documents of Portugal's two stored routes, `pt-d7` and `pt-d8` ([#525](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/525), spec [#519](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/519)), and version 2 of Brazil's document guide ([ADR-0039](../../../../docs/adr/0039-document-guides.md)). Version 8 of each corrects version 7 from its date, 2026-01-01: it keeps every item of version 7 and adds the route's official document list, `documents` ([ADR-0032](../../../../docs/adr/0032-session-reviews-activate-through-a-reviewed-pull-request.md), [runbook](../../../../docs/runbooks/session-rule-review.md#documents)). Every source the documents cite was read on 2026-11-25, São Paulo date.

## Where the lists come from

Portugal's official-domain list holds one host, `diariodarepublica.pt`, and the consulates' and AIMA's pages (`vistos.mne.gov.pt`, `aima.gov.pt`) refuse this session. So the lists come from the regulation itself, Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, which sets the documents of every visa application: its republication annexed to Decreto Regulamentar n.º 4/2022, and the paragraphs Decreto Regulamentar n.º 1/2024 rewrote in full (articles 10.º, n.º 1, 12.º, n.º 1, a) and c), and 24.º-B, n.º 1, a)). The D7 means proofs come from Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 5.º, n.º 6.

## What version 8 adds

**Everyone who applies** (the principal and each family member, who files a residence visa of their own under article 58.º, n.º 5, of Lei n.º 23/2007): the application on its own form, signed by the legal representative for a minor or an incapable person (article 10.º, n.os 1 and 3); two photographs or a digital facial image; a passport or other valid travel document, which must be valid at least three months beyond the planned stay to enter Portugal (article 4.º); the criminal record certificate of the country of nationality or of residence for more than a year, from age 16 (article 12.º, n.º 1, c), and n.º 7); travel insurance with medical cover and repatriation, which an applicant who cannot obtain one may be spared (n.º 1, e), and n.º 6); and the authorization for a minor or an adult under a protective measure (n.º 5).

**Each family member** (article 24.º-B, n.º 1): proof of the family ties, and proof of the family's stable and regular resources for the stay or 12 months, whichever is shorter.

**`pt-d7`, the principal:** a retiree's proof of the pension and of its guaranteed payment, or of other income available in Portugal; or, for income from property, intellectual property or financial investments, proof of that income, its amount and its availability in Portugal, both for means assured for at least 12 months (article 12.º, n.º 1, f), and Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 5.º, n.º 6). Both may apply: no case fact tells a pension from other passive income.

**`pt-d8`, the principal** (article 31.º-A, n.º 1): an employment contract or employer's statement, or a company contract, contract for services or record of services provided (each may apply, as no case fact says which); proof of the average monthly income of the last three months, at least four minimum wages; a document attesting tax residence; and the general proof of means (article 12.º, n.º 1, f)).

Conditions: the criminal record reads `person.ageYears` from 16; the authorization applies under 18 and may apply to an adult, since no fact records a protective measure; the age of 18 rests on the means scale, as the child rules do. No item sets an apostille, a translation or a validity: no text read states one.

**Brazil's document guide, version 2** (`packages/knowledge/document-guides/2.json`, wording in `pt-BR-document-guides.ts`): version 1, plus the INSS benefit payment statement (`pension_statement`, Meu INSS) and the Receita Federal's tax residence certificate (`tax_residence_certificate`, Atestado de Residência Fiscal no Brasil). Domains unchanged: `gov.br` and `jus.br`.

## Rounds

Two adversarial rounds: one reviewer checked every item, its quotes, its pt-BR text and the two new guide entries against the texts; one looked for missing or out-of-date documents.

- Round 1: the item reviewer reported 6 findings and the gap reviewer 2, besides points it checked and cleared. Fixed: `family-means` quoted article 24.º-B, n.º 1, whole from 2022, with a point a) that Decreto Regulamentar n.º 1/2024 rewrote, and now quotes point b) alone; `family-ties` claimed the "accompanies" context its 2024 quote does not hold, and is now "Proof of the family ties"; the article 4.º quote now runs to its end, with both exceptions; the D8 income text in pt-BR said "salários mínimos", read in Brazil as Brazil's, and now says Portugal's "remunerações mínimas mensais garantidas"; the tax residence wording now names the gov.br account level its page asks for. Two findings went to the leftover list with their reason: the age of 18 for the minor's authorization (7) and the other parent's consent (5). Not changed: the CPLP exemption, already leftover 3; the termo de responsabilidade, the kinship examination of article 13.º and the accommodation field of article 11.º, none of them a document every applicant hands in.
- Round 2 read those fixes and leftovers 3, 5 and 7 and found nothing wrong. Two pt-BR wordings changed after it, from it and the repository's standards reviewer, checked only by the session and the tests: the D8 income text ("no valor de pelo menos quatro") and the insurance note ("contratar esse seguro").

The quote check, the app's own (`ARCA_REVIEW_QUOTES=2026-11-pt-documents`, in `apps/api`), passed in the session on 2026-11-25 UTC.

## Leftover

1. **The consulates' own list is not read.** `vistos.mne.gov.pt` and `aima.gov.pt` refuse this session and are not on Portugal's official-domain list, which only the app can extend. What a consulate asks beyond the regulation (such as proof of accommodation, a Portuguese tax number or bank account) is not listed.
2. **No apostille, translation or validity.** No text read says whether a Brazilian document needs an apostille or a translation, or how recent a criminal record certificate must be; the checklist shows each as not known.
3. **The CPLP residence visa.** A national of a state where the CPLP mobility agreement is in force may file under article 12.º, n.º 3 (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 1/2024), without the documents specific to each type of visa, the insurance, and, with a statement of responsibility, the proof of means; such a file gives a CPLP residence visa (article 24.º-A, n.º 1), a different visa that no stored route models. Which states the agreement is in force for was not read on an official page.
4. **Family ties are one item.** Article 24.º-B asks for proof of the ties without naming the document, and the case cannot tell a spouse's edge from a child's in a document condition, so no item asks for a marriage or birth certificate by name, and Brazil's guide has no entry for `family_relationship_proof`.
5. **Proofs the accompanying visa does not list.** Article 67.º lists, for family reunification (article 98.º), proof of dependence for a parent or an adult child, an adult child's school enrolment, an adult child's incapacity, the other parent's consent and the proof of a de facto union. Article 24.º-B, which governs the family members who apply with the principal, names none of them, so they are not listed. The other parent's consent, or a custody decision, for a child of only one spouse is article 99.º, n.º 4, of Lei n.º 23/2007; article 58.º, n.º 5, brings in only n.º 1 of article 99.º, and the child rules already say the consent is not checked.
6. **Income from work for a D7 principal.** Article 12.º-A, n.º 1, counts means from a work or services contract, which version 7 reads as possible; no document item asks for such a contract.
7. **The age of 18 for the minor's authorization** rests on the means scale (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, article 2.º, n.º 2, c)), as version 7's child rules do: article 12.º, n.º 5, says only "menor", and the Código Civil's age of majority is in a gazette scan with no text.
8. **Lei n.º 23/2007, article 52.º, n.º 1, g),** asks for a minor's parental authorization only when no one with parental responsibilities goes along; the regulation's article 12.º, n.º 5, asks for it on every minor's application, and the item follows the regulation.
9. **Brazil's guide does not cover** the visa form, photographs, travel insurance, the minor's authorization, proof of passive income, the proof of means, work contracts, income evidence or the family ties: no Brazilian office issues them as such, or no official page read says where.
10. **Amendments.** The gazette search for "Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007" and "Portaria n.º 1563/2007" returned no act after Decreto Regulamentar n.º 1/2024; the 2026-11-20 review read the amending lists up to Diário da República n.º 218/2026 of 2026-11-10.
11. **The 2027 minimum wage** ([#220](https://github.com/fernandolisboa/arca/issues/220)) is not stored yet: it will be version 9 of both routes, from 2027-01-01, and must keep version 8's documents.
12. **Production rows were not read.** This session has no database credentials; the proof of publishing is the production build, which runs the migration.
